Getting started — first-time setup, 5 steps
  1. Chat logs on. In EVE: ESC → Chat → Log Chat to File. The radar reads Local's log to know which toons you run and where they are. Jump once after turning it on.
  2. Place the overlay. EVE must run windowed or Fixed window. Press Ctrl+Shift+O, drag the mini-map where you want it, resize from the edges, press again to lock (it's click-through when locked).
  3. Your systems and ships. Under Targets (below) list the systems you camp or hunt. Under Watched ships pick the hulls you care about: ✓ = highlight, ✗ = also beep.
  4. Pilot lookup. Mark Local area and drag a box over Local's member list. Then middle-click any name in Local for a zKill card.
  5. Optional: jump toons. Log in your blops/capital toons under Jump drive toons to see which systems they reach.
Everything saves automatically. Click ⓘ Legend on the map for what the colours mean.

Jump drive toons (ESI)

Log in your blops / capital toons. While ESI says one sits in a jump-drive hull, the mini-map shows ⤳ In range: … (which of them can reach the toon you're flying), alerts get ⤳✓, and in-range systems get a dashed violet ring. Range uses each toon's real Jump Drive Calibration level.
How to set up the ESI login (once)
  1. Go to developers.eveonline.com, log in and create a new application.
  2. Callback URL: http://localhost:8765/callback
  3. Scopes: esi-location.read_ship_type.v1 and esi-skills.read_skills.v1 — nothing else is needed.
  4. Copy the app's Client ID, paste it below and press Save.
  5. Log in a character opens EVE's login page in your browser — pick the toon, accept. Repeat for each jump toon.
The radar only reads ship type and skills. It can't move, fit or see anything else on the account.

Pilot lookup

Set up: Mark Local area, then drag a box over Local's member list (redo it if you move or resize Local).
Use: middle-click a name in Local → a card with zKill danger, recent kills and links. The click still reaches EVE; only that one row is read.
Tip: click in Local and press Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C once per system. That fills Local below with every pilot and their danger, and makes name reading much more accurate.

Best nearby

Lowsec and nullsec within your watch range (jumps) of the character you're flying, ranked by ratting (NPC kills/h), recent watched-ship kills and PvP.

Local

Fills when you copy Local (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C in Local). Sorted by danger; ⚠ = seen killing in a watched ship.

Watched ships

In the editor, click a box to cycle: ✓ watched (highlighted on the map and in kill popups, silent) → ✗ pinged (also beeps) → off. Several named lists can be on at once.

Watch range

Kills outside this range are ignored completely (not on the map, no heat, no beeps). Highsec and wormholes are never watched. Bigger range = more on the map, a bit more network traffic.

Beeps

Two sounds: a low klaxon = any fight close to you, a bright beep = a ✗ pinged ship. Everything else is silent (map + popups only).
